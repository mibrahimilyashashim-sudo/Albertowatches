INSIDE THE TIMEPIECE

Understanding Watch Movements

Different movement systems use different methods to keep track of time.

Mechanical Movement

Uses a system of springs and gears to power the watch. Many mechanical watches are valued for their intricate engineering and traditional craftsmanship.

Quartz Movement

Uses an electronic circuit and a vibrating quartz crystal to regulate timekeeping. Quartz watches commonly operate using a battery.

Smartwatch Technology

Combines electronic timekeeping with digital displays and, depending on the model, connectivity and activity-related features.
