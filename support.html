Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Vintage, Luxury, and Smart Watches.

What types of watches do you sell?

Our store specializes in three categories of watches: Vintage Watches, Luxury Watches, and Smart Watches. Each collection is carefully selected for customers who value style, quality, and uniqueness.

What makes your vintage watches special?

Our vintage watches feature classic designs inspired by different eras. They are perfect for customers who appreciate timeless style, traditional craftsmanship, and the charm of vintage fashion.

Are your luxury watches authentic?

Yes, we focus on offering high-quality and authentic luxury watches. Product details and specifications are provided with each watch so customers can make a confident purchase.

What features do your smart watches offer?

Smart watches offer useful features such as fitness tracking, step counting, heart-rate monitoring, smartphone notifications, and activity tracking. Depending on the model, some watches may also include sleep tracking, alarms, and customizable watch faces. These features combine everyday convenience with modern style.
